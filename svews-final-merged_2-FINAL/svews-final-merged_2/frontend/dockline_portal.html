Categories

Three catalogues, one warehouse

Every supplier is grouped into a physical-goods category, each scored by the same risk engine with category-aware features.

Medical / Pharmacy

Over-the-counter medicine, first-aid, and wellness items with a strict non-returnable policy on medicines.

Stationery

School and office supplies — books, pens, art and craft essentials — sourced from local stationers.

Electronics

Mobile accessories, home electronics, and computer peripherals from vetted local retailers.

How it works

Risk scoring, built into the flow

The ML model isn't a bolt-on report — it runs the moment a supplier joins, and refreshes automatically as they trade.

01

Supplier registers

A supplier signs up under Medical, Stationery, or Electronics and lists their catalogue.

02

Model scores instantly

The ONNX-based XGBoost model evaluates 14 features and returns a live risk category at registration — not a placeholder default.

03

Metrics stay live

9 of 14 features auto-refresh nightly from real orders, deliveries, and disputes — so the score reflects current behaviour.

04

Admins get early warning

Suppliers drifting toward the Watchlist tier are surfaced to admins before it affects customers.

Platform features

Built as a complete marketplace

Beyond risk scoring, SVEWS covers the full buy-and-sell lifecycle for a physical-goods warehouse.

Live risk engine

XGBoost via ONNX runtime, scoring every supplier's viability across 14 operational features.

Secure checkout

Simulated card payment kept entirely in-browser, plus Cash on Delivery — card details never reach the server.

7-day returns

Standard 7-day return window, with medicines explicitly excluded and earnings adjusted on reversal.

Wishlist & reviews

Verified-purchase reviews and a saved wishlist for every shopper, plus supplier coupon codes.

Low-stock alerts

Suppliers and admins are notified automatically once stock drops below 10 units.

Fraud detection

Duplicate-account checks across IP, phone, catalogue similarity, and login-timing correlation.
